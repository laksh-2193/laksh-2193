~/index · /about

Builder. Author.
Reluctant futurist.

Engineer at the seam where language models meet production systems. I write code, I write books, and I run my mouth on stages — usually about why agents need ops too.

I build AI agents that ship. Not the demo-reel kind — the kind that operate inside enterprise systems with real users, real audit logs, and real stakes.

Currently GenAI Engineer at Zato, where I lead agent architecture across our document intelligence (DocIQ) and operations copilot (OMS) products. Before that, I cut my teeth on data science, computer vision on drones, and Web3 health records.

I'm also an author — From Data to World is my attempt to write a data-science book that doesn't read like a tutorial. I speak at meetups (Microsoft Reactor, ThoughtWorks, Solapur FOSS, TinyML), mentor through Microsoft's MLSA program, and run education projects at Atria Foundation.

Outside of work I read systems papers and rebuild old ideas in modern stacks — to see what changes when the building blocks get an order of magnitude better.
